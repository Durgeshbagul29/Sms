Product Overview

A single, auditable record for every safety report

SMS Safety Management System is a mobile-first safety management platform for reporting, reviewing and resolving safety observations, accidents and near-misses — with live KPIs and export-ready reports built in from day one.

Full Lifecycle

Built for the full lifecycle, not just the form

Most safety registers stop at "collect the form." SMS Safety Management System carries every report from first capture through to a closed, reportable record.

Reports move through one queue instead of a paper register, so nothing is filed and forgotten. Every status change is timestamped, every decision is logged against the account that made it, and every record stays exportable for the next audit.

1. Capture

Report from the app or web portal in minutes, with a photo and short description.

2. Review

Admins see every submission in one queue and act with a reason on file.

3. Notify

The reporter hears the moment status changes — no chasing supervisors.

4. Report

KPIs and auditable PDF/Excel exports are one click away, always.

Why SMS Safety Management System

Safety registers break down at scale

Paper forms and standalone spreadsheets work for a handful of reports. Past that, visibility disappears and hazards stay open.

Without SMS Safety

  • Reports sit in a register or inbox with no one accountable for closing them.
  • Reporters have no way to know if their observation was actioned or forgotten.
  • Evidence photos live on someone's phone, not attached to the record.
  • A monthly safety report means manually collating data from every department.
  • There's no single, auditable trail of who resolved what, and when.

With SMS Safety Management System

  • Every report enters one queue, routed straight to the right admin for action.
  • Real-time status updates and notifications tell the reporter exactly where things stand.
  • Photo evidence is stored on the record itself, ready for reference at any time.
  • Auditable Excel and PDF reports for any timeframe are generated in a click.
  • Every update, approval and escalation is logged against the account that made it.

Access & Governance

Five Roles, Tailored for Enterprise EHS Governance

Every account sees exactly what its role requires — from shop-floor workers to executive directors.

Role 3 · Employee

Employee / Shop-Floor Worker

Frontline participation: report safety observations, near-misses, accidents, and confidential ethics complaints with evidence.

  • Create & submit safety observations, near-misses & accidents
  • Attach photo & video evidence (up to 50MB boundary)
  • Submit confidential ethics & whistleblower reports
  • Receive instant FCM push alerts & track status in real time
Role 5 · Manager

Department Manager

Department-level supervision: first-level operational review, evidence inspection, and team safety oversight.

  • First-level departmental review of safety submissions
  • Inspect evidence, approve, reject, or request revisions
  • Send targeted departmental announcements & push alerts
  • Export department-scoped safety logs & reports
Role 1 · EHS Admin

EHS Safety Admin

Company-wide EHS administration: final status progression, status verification, plant KPI analytics, and audit exports.

  • Company-wide review queue & final status verification
  • Progress status (Pending → Approved → Implemented & Closed)
  • Monitor plant-wide EHS KPIs & trend charts
  • Generate auditable Excel & single-incident PDF reports
Role 2 · HR Admin

HR Administrator

User administration & organizational governance: managing accounts, role provisioning, and department mapping.

  • Provision accounts for Employees, Managers, Admins & Directors
  • Manage department structures & assign department managers
  • Reset passwords & manage user status (Active/Deactivated)
  • Review & action confidential ethics & whistleblower reports
Role 4 · Director

Director / Executive Management

Strategic visibility: executive-level EHS monitoring, plant trend analytics, and audit oversight without changing operational statuses.

  • Executive dashboard with company-wide safety KPIs
  • Analyze historical trends for observations, near-misses & accidents
  • Audit visibility into compliance performance across plants
  • Generate executive EHS summary reports

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything plant safety officers, EHS leaders, and IT teams ask before deploying SMS Safety Management System.

The platform supports 5 distinct role-based portals: Employee (Role 3: shop-floor reporting), Department Manager (Role 5: first-level review & team CAPA), EHS Safety Admin (Role 1: company-wide verification & reporting), HR Administrator (Role 2: account provisioning & department assignment), and Executive Director (Role 4: executive trend monitoring).
SMS Safety Management System automates statutory reporting under the Indian Factories Act, 1948 and aligns with international ISO 45001 occupational health and safety standards. It creates immutable, timestamped records of every safety observation, accident investigation, and root cause analysis, allowing safety officers to export audit-ready Excel and PDF dossiers in one click.
When a hazard, near miss, or accident is reported, Department Managers and EHS Admins assign dedicated corrective ownership, set target completion timelines, and track remediation progress. Remedial teams upload before-and-after photographic evidence, and the original reporting employee receives an automated notification upon verified closure.
Each module tracks specific status codes: Observations move from Pending (0) to Approved (1) or Rejected (2); Near Misses and Accidents move from Pending/Investigation (0) to Implemented & Closed (1); and Ethics Complaints progress from Pending (0) to Seen/Reviewed (1).
Yes! Reporters can attach photo evidence and video clips up to 50MB directly from their mobile app or web browser to document hazards, root causes, and before/after corrective actions.
Yes. Employees can submit confidential ethics complaints covering workplace misconduct, harassment, fraud, or policy violations. Submissions are stored securely and reviewed exclusively by authorized HR and administrative roles.
Both. Download a detailed PDF report for any single incident from submission through closure with full status history, or generate a consolidated Excel report across all parameters for any date range in one click.
Through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), push notifications are automatically delivered to registered mobile devices the moment a report status changes or when Admins and Managers issue company-wide or departmental safety announcements.
A Safety Observation is an unsafe act, unsafe condition, or positive safety practice spotted on site. A Near Miss is a hazardous event that almost caused harm but did not result in an accident. Both surface hazards early for preventive action.
User accounts are administratively managed by HR Administrators. HR provisions credentials for Employees, Managers, Admins, and Directors, maps managers to departments, resets passwords, and manages active/deactivated statuses.
