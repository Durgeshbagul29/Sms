All successful companies want to create a workplace that is Safe, healthy and reliable. A Good workplace safety program is based on different types of incident reporting, and the employee's knowledge of when, where, and how to submit the report of the Incident. Incident reports are a well-established idea that are Observations/Accidents/Near-Miss that are encountered by the employees/Workers in the company.

How can a Company manage all these Incident reporting to create a safe workplace? By Integrating a Safety Management System into the Company Processes to monitor every Incident and Take right and timely action against each of them. What is a Safety Management System?

A Safety management system is a systematic approach to managing safety, including organisational structures, accountabilities, policies and procedures. It records all the Incident that happened during the processes and helps in implementing changes to avoid them in the future.

An SMS gives employees a powerful mobile tool to document reports about incidents, accidents, injuries and more by themselves. A Safety Management system acts as a centralised hub for all the Reports which then can be used for error correcting and Safety Auditing. One of the benefits is the time savings the company heads get from on-site documentation in the event of any incident that takes place in the Company Premise.

Importance of a Safety Management System In any company

Safety management system by AbracaDabra Software solution can help a company employee report the Incidents by themselves and Also provide solutions that can be implemented for a better change. Safety management system software eliminates the need of filling out paperwork and creating lengthy reports to maintain the Records of Incidents.

Safety Management System is the best software for Safety Management. It has incredible functionality that Provides users with wide range of features packed in beautiful design.

Core Features of SMS

Auditable reporting, real-time KPI metrics, automated notifications, and multi-platform accessibility across iOS, Android, and Web.

The major features of Safety Management System are:

  • Auditable Reporting: Software offers excel based auditable report for any time frame for n number of safety management system user.
  • Safety KPI: Safety KPI tells exact picture about total number of Safety Observations/Accidents/Near-Miss Updated by admins in a particular timeframe.
  • Realtime Update: All Safety Observations/Accidents/Near-Miss and their statuses are updated in real time. Updating of Safety Observations/Accidents/Near-Miss are intimated.
  • Notification: If your Safety Observations /Accidents/Near-Miss is update or safety status is changed by admin then appropriate notifications are send to the appropriate user. And user can see the notification.

Safety management system also let the user visualise the Data for easier Understanding. The system is Simple and works on multiple platform such as IOS and Android and also has a Web client.
